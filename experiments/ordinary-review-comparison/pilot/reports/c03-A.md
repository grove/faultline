  Two small regression checks would protect the intended behavior:
  1. With `Timer(auto=True)`, `ttl=2`, and one inserted item, assert `[(key, cache[key]) for key in cache]` returns its pair. Without the timer context spanning the yield, lookup advances to the deadline and raises `KeyError`.
  2. Insert two items one tick apart with `ttl=2`. Start iterating while both are live, consume the first, advance the underlying timer to the second item’s exact deadline, and assert the next call raises `StopIteration`. This catches freezing one timestamp for the entire traversal and yielding newly expired entries.
